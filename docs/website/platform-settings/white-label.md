# White Label Branding [PRO]

Make the marketplace yours. White Label removes WP Sell Services branding and applies your own across the buyer and vendor experience.

## What you can brand

- **Marketplace name and logo**: shown in the vendor dashboard header and transactional emails.
- **Brand color**: applied to dashboard accents and email headers.
- **Admin menu label**: rename "Services" in wp-admin to anything (e.g. your platform name).
- **Powered-by attribution**: remove it with one toggle.

## Setup

1. Go to **Services > Settings > Branding**.
2. Upload a logo, set the brand name and color, save.
3. Emails and the frontend dashboard pick the branding up immediately. No template edits.

## For agencies

White Label plus the Agency license is built for client builds: ship a marketplace under the client's brand with nothing to relabel later.
