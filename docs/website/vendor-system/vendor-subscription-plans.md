# Vendor Subscription Plans [PRO]

Charge vendors for the right to sell. Create plans that gate how many services a vendor can list and what features they get.

## Create plans

1. Go to **Services > Settings > Vendor Subscriptions**.
2. Add plans with a name, monthly or yearly price, and limits (number of active services, featured slots).
3. Plans bill through Stripe.

## What enforcement does

A vendor on the Starter plan with a 3-service limit cannot publish a 4th service. The wizard explains the limit and links to the upgrade flow. Expired subscriptions move vendor services back to draft after the grace period.

## Combining with commissions

Plans work alongside commissions. Common models: lower commission on higher plans, or free listings with commission-only on the entry plan. Pair with Tiered Commission Rules for per-plan rates.
