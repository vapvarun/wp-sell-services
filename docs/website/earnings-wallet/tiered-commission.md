# Tiered Commission Rules [PRO]

Replace the single global commission rate with rules: by vendor, by category, by seller level, or by sales volume.

## Create rules

1. Go to **Services > Settings > Commissions**.
2. Add a rule: choose what it matches (vendor, category, seller level, volume threshold) and the rate.
3. Order rules by priority. The first matching rule wins; the global rate stays as the fallback.

## Examples

- Top Rated sellers pay 10 percent instead of 20.
- The Design category pays 15 percent.
- A specific enterprise vendor pays a flat 5 percent.
- Vendors over 10k in monthly sales drop to 12 percent.

Earnings, the vendor ledger, and analytics all respect the resolved per-order rate, so reporting stays accurate.
