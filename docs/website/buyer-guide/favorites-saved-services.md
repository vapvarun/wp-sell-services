# Favorites: Save Services for Later

Found a service you like but not ready to order? Save it to your favorites and come back when you are.

## Saving a service

Tap the heart on any service card in the marketplace, or use the favorite button on the service page itself. The heart fills in to confirm the save. Tap again to remove it.

You need an account to save favorites. Logged-out visitors get a login prompt when they tap the heart.

## Finding your favorites

Open your dashboard and go to the **Favorites** section. Every saved service is listed with its current price and availability, so you can compare options before committing to an order.

From the list you can:

- Open the service page to order it
- Remove services you are no longer considering

## Why favorites beat browser bookmarks

Favorites stay attached to your account, follow you across devices, and always show the live price. If a vendor pauses a service or goes on vacation, you see its real status instead of a dead link.
